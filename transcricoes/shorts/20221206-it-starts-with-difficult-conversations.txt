It Starts with Difficult Conversations

You know, we teach people the skills how to do their jobs. We teach business school students how to read a P&L. But what we're not doing a good job of is teaching leadership. We teach management, but we don't teach leadership. And leadership is that very human skill that awesome responsibility to see those around us rise. That's what leadership is. And that requires human skills. I despise, I detest the term soft skills. When we talk about hard skills and soft skills, those things are opposite. This is hard skills, the skills I need to do my job better, and human skills, the skills I need to be a better human and thus a better leader. And you need both. Human skills include things like listening. We don't teach listening. But we can teach the skill set on how to have difficult conversations. Oh, and by the way, if you can have a difficult conversation about race, you can have a difficult conversation about pretty much anything that happens at work.

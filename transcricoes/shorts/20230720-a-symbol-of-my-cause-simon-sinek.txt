So, I got this question recently. What is symbolic about the color orange? And if you know my work from over the years, you'll always notice that there's always a little bit of orange somewhere on my person. Well, the reason is simple. They're reminders. I am human and sometimes I forget my own cause, I forget my own why, I forget why I'm doing this, I get sucked into the details of the daily grind, and by looking at the color orange, it reminds me to be bright and optimistic. And so, it's a symbol of my cause, it's a symbol of what I believe, and it serves as a little bit of a reminder to me as well. Orange. Love orange.

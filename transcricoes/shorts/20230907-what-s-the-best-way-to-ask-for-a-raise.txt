What's the best way to ask for a raise?

How do I start a difficult conversation regarding pay? Somebody comes in and says, "I deserve this amount and these are the reasons why." Or, "Look how hard I've been working, I deserve money." And very often they put a number out there. When that happens, it's backing somebody into a corner. In other words, they're setting up a yes or no, which is very difficult. What I find is way more productive is to talk to somebody and say, "May I talk to you about my pay? May I talk to you about my compensation?" Like, set it up because conversations about money are often very uncomfortable. So, you're setting up an uncomfortable conversation. You can have numbers in your mind of what you'd like to achieve, that's totally fine. What you want to do is create a dialogue. And very often these conversations go south because they become a demand rather than a dialogue. They become something that is proposed as a yes or no, um, and it becomes much more heated and much more emotional. So, give it a try. Try and make it a dialogue where you can have a conversation about how to get on a path to get more compensation in the short, medium, and long term.

Small Change ➡️  Big Improvements

Um one of my favorite ones is having a negative mindset versus a positive mindset. So, when somebody says, "I'm no good at that." I always say add the word yet at the end. "I'm no good at that yet." Or "I can't do that yet." Or "I suck at that for now." Right? And instead of making those insecure declarations final, just add a little bit of doubt to the negativity. You start to find that you have a more positive point of view on what's possible. And I like doing it for other people when I catch somebody saying, "Well, I I don't know how to do that." And I go, "Yet." You don't know how to do that yet. Or you suck at that right now. So, that that to me is very easy to do and it pays dividends over time.

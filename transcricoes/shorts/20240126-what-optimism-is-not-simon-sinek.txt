What Optimism is NOT | Simon Sinek

When I describe myself as an optimist, people's immediate reaction is that I'm naive or blind. Optimism is neither. It is not to be confused with blind positivity. Everything's fine, everything's good, you know, or the obsession of focusing on the things that are working and ignoring the things that are broken. Optimism is is very realistic. It allows for frustration and sadness, but it's the undying belief that the future is bright. So, an optimist would say we are in a dark tunnel. I do not know how long we're going to be in here and it will be difficult, but I know one thing for sure. If we work together and take care of each other, we will get through this and we will come out of this stronger than we went in.

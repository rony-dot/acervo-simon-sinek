I was struggling to trust people... | Simon Sinek

I was going through a period in my own career where I was struggling to trust people. Everybody keeps telling us trust is important, but they don't tell us what trust is or how it's built. Simultaneously, I just happened to be spending time with folks in the military, and I kept meeting these people who would willingly risk their lives to save the lives of people they didn't even like. Whereas in business, you know, we don't want to even want to give up credit for things, let alone our lives. I started asking the question, how do I get people like that at work? My original theory was that they're just better people, and better people are drawn to this life of service. And the more I started to learn, the more I started to investigate, the more I realized I was completely wrong. It's not actually about the person, it's about the environment. You can take a good person and put them in a bad environment, and they will do bad things. Conversely, you can take someone who may have been perceived as untrustworthy, you can change their environment, and they can change their lives and become valuable members of a team. Leaders set the environment, so if you can create environment, biologically, trust will follow.

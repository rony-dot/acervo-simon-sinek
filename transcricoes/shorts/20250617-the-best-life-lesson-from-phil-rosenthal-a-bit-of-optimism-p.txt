The Best Life Lesson from PHIL ROSENTHAL | A Bit of Optimism #Podcast

If you're going to have to beat your head against a wall, pick something you like doing and beat your head against that wall. What made you want to start an eating show in the first place? After Everybody Loves Raymond was over, I had been there for nine years. The business changed greatly in those nine years. They didn't want that kind of show anymore. They wanted hip and edgy shows. I wouldn't know how to do the hip and edgy, right? Young hip sitcom. The best advice I ever got from anybody was from Ed Weinberger, a great show creator and showrunner. I asked him for advice. He says this, "Do the show you want to do because in the end they're going to cancel you anyway." That's a life lesson. We all get cancelled one day. So live the life you want to live.
